## User-selected direction and closer ocean references

The user selected Hack the North 2024 for illustration and collage, and KIKK for bold typography and clean schedule structure. The Deep Sea is not a primary reference: its sea-exploration purpose is too different from an ocean-themed event schedule. Do not infer approval for a depth-based schedule from the earlier proposal.

Closer references researched on September 7, 2026:

- https://www.deluxburyseafarm.com/ — strongest new reference to inspect. Browser-verified farm section shows paper texture, rough section edges, rope-like borders, and illustrated nautical navigation alongside ordinary content. The Awwwards listing https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/desktop-deluxbury-sea-farm also identifies animated hand-drawn illustration; detailed motion behavior has not been tested. Suggested borrowing: nautical frames and small illustrations that participate in page structure, while keeping KIKK typography.
- https://eatfishwife.com/ — browser-verified shop combines a cream reading panel and outlined controls with colorful illustrated seafood packaging. Suggested borrowing: concentrate artwork and color in selected objects, leaving text and controls readable. It is a shop, not a schedule.

The studio describes a nautical restaurant website and a library of Victorian-style sea-life illustrations. Useful for restrained maritime motifs. Live-site behavior and full case-study visuals not checked.
